# gfx1201 — RX 9070 PR-ready: dense Qwen3.5, performance, and coherent output

## Goal

Take branch `gfx1201` (HEAD `4e82eaf`) to a PR-ready state: the RX 9070 (gfx1201,
RDNA4) is a supported standalone device for dense Qwen3.5-family models, and the 9B
fixture is not just numerically correct but **fast enough to use** and produces
coherent, functional output.

This plan now also requires:

- **Performance.** The 9B-Ora is currently ~16.8 tok/s on the 9070. Close the gap to
  the same pack on upstream exllamav3 (measurable baseline) and to this engine's
  codebook-pack throughput. Prefer changes that are generic: they must plausibly
  benefit `Qwen3.8-27B`, `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next`, `GLM-5.3-Flash`,
  `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`, and general dense/MoE inference, not a 9B-only special case.
- **Coherence.** No looping/degenerate repetition on the 9070. A usable chat path
  (template + stop conditions + optional loop guard) and a cross-device quality gate.
- **No regressions.** gfx1151 inference must not regress. The RTX 3090/CUDA path is
  not testable here, so it must be untouched by construction (ROCm-only code,
  `torch.version.hip`-gated Python, no default-behavior changes on CUDA).

Layer split remains the sibling plan `gfx1201-layersplit-continue`; this plan only
uses a split as a secondary route-coverage check for non-fitting models.

## Why 16.8 tok/s is expected (code-checked)

- The OraQuant pack stores no `mcg`/`mul1` tensors, so its linear layers are `cb=0`
  (plain procedural codebook).
- The HIP GEMV selector instantiates `cb=0` only for 4 bpw:
  `exllamav3/exllamav3_ext/quant/exl3_gemv.cu:1326-1332` (`SEL_GRID(4, 0)` and no
  `(2,0)/(3,0)/(5,0)/(6,0)`), and rejects `K != 4 && cb == 0` at `:1279` and `:1416`.
- The Python route likewise admits plain only at `K == 4`:
  `exllamav3/modules/quant/exl3.py:517` (`(self.K == 4 or self.mcg or self.mul1)`).
- Therefore the pack's K2/K3/K5 projections legitimately reconstruct fp16 weights on
  every token (`reconstruct + hgemm`), while only its K4 tensors take the GEMV. The
  codebook-pack 9B routes every K to the GEMV, a major reason it is ~3x faster.
- This also limits the current oracle: both arms share the K2/K3/K5 reconstruct path,
  so it cannot detect a bug shared by `reconstruct` and the fallback, and it only
  compares 16 forced steps. A per-token full-weight reconstruction is the dominant
  cost and a plausible source of drift/looping.

## Hypotheses to test (do not assume the fix)

- **H1 (perf, primary).** Closing plain-codebook (`cb=0`) GEMV coverage for K2/K3/K5/K6
  removes the per-token reconstruction and recovers most of the 3x gap on the Ora pack.
  This is generic for every EXL3 pack without a codebook, on gfx1201 **and** gfx1151.
- **H2 (perf).** `exl3_gemv_cfg`'s shape heuristics (tuned for CUDA/gfx11.5) reject hot
  K3/wide shapes, and the gfx1201 config choice leaves bandwidth on the table for the
  codebook packs (`Qwen3.8-27B`, `GLM-5.3-Flash`, etc.).
- **H3 (perf).** After H1, the two extra Hadamard launches per linear and the
  single-weight GEMV launch are a material part of the step; multi-weight fusion
  (gate+up, qkv, qkvz) is a generic lever for dense models.
- **H4 (quality).** The loop is not a gfx12 kernel bug. It may reproduce on gfx1151 or
  upstream exllamav3 (pack/model/template issue), or it may be a gfx12-only route bug
  in GDN/attention/rope/rmsnorm, or a long-horizon drift not visible in 16 forced steps.
  The first three tests below distinguish these before touching code.

## Phase 1 — Performance workstream

## Caveats / follow-ups (2026-11-03)

1. **Upstream perf gap.** 58.2 tok/s vs upstream v1.6.6's 89.8 on the same Ora pack
   and RX 9070. Small-N GEMVs reach 110-155 GB/s (K5 lm_head 356 GB/s), so the
   kernel is latency/occupancy-bound on 128 CUs. A new split-K or finer-N gfx12
   config is the likely lever; `cfg 0/1` (P2) and multi-weight fusion (P3) were not
   justified by measurement.
2. **[FIXED] ROCm skinny hgemm grid bug (m=9..32, n<=128).** The CPL=2 grid was
   used with the one-column padded kernels, leaving columns 64..127 unwritten and
   making reconstruct nondeterministic for rows 9..32. Fixed in `hgemm.cu` by
   restricting CPL=2 to m<=8. The GEMV at n=128 was always bit-identical to
   `reconstruct + torch.matmul`; the MMODE-2/K5 synthetic tests at n=128 now pass.
   See `doc/gfx1201-cb0.md`.
3. **Pre-existing test failures, reproduced on the baseline `.so`:**
   `test_hip_routing_std_gfx12` kernel-name profiling (ROCm profiler exposes no
   device kernel names), `test_hip_block_graph` rows-decline counter, and
   `test_hip_grouped_moe_k3` (`ext.exl3_moe_gfx12_k3` TypeError, 89 cases).
4. **Multi-device JIT arch.** `vendor/fla/hip/gdn_hf_hip.py`,
   `gdn_fused_h_hip.py`, and the `gr_mix` compile path still take device 0's
   `gcnArchName`; `hip_compiler.py` has no device argument. Default-off on family 1,
   but unsafe if enabled with the dGPU at a nonzero index.
5. **Fixture availability.** Qwen3.8-27B and the 9B MCG fixture are not on the halo
   (`/data/models` has Ora, Flash-Next, GLM-5.3 only). Their acceptance-matrix rows
   are coverage only; no downloads were triggered. Large-pack coverage was checked
   from safetensors headers (all mul1, K3-K6; Flash-Next K8 falls back).
6. **gfx11.5-only kernels.** `exl3_dec`, `qdec.hip`, `gr_mix` stay pinned to family 2;
   quantized KV on gfx1201 still falls back to Triton (correct, slow). Vision remains
   out of scope.
7. **Keep the 16-step tight delta bounds.** 128-step logit deltas amplify through the
   hybrid GDN recurrence on both devices; the oracle keeps top1 identity as the hard
   long-trace signal and scales delta bounds with `EXL3_ORACLE_STEPS`.

## Risks / notes

- **Plain K5/K6 may reveal a latent codebook bug.** The current oracle cannot see it
  because both arms share reconstruct. Treat any GEMV-vs-reconstruct disagreement as
  a codebook/reconstruct bug until proven otherwise; keep that K/cb on reconstruct.
- **Build time/size.** More instantiations cost compile time and `.so` size. Start
  with the m=1 set; add rows>1 only when a measured path needs it.
- **K3/wide heuristics.** Forcing a GEMV that loses to reconstruct is a regression;
  only extend `exl3_gemv_cfg` for a reproducible win.
- **Looping may be the pack.** The Ora pack may be degenerate; do not paper over it
  with a repetition penalty or a silently enabled guard. Swap the quality fixture and
  document it.
- **Multi-device JIT arch.** `gdn_*_hip.py` and `gr_mix`-class kernels must not compile
  for device 0's arch when the target is device 1; keep them off family 1 until the
  probe is fixed.
- **16 GB card.** The 27B may only fit with a small cache; Flash Next/GLM/DSV4 need
  the split flow. Their validation here is eligibility/measurement, not a full run.
- **The venv has no pip**; use `uv pip`. The editable install points at the exllamav3
  tree, so `PYTHONPATH` to the kyojin root wins and the `--target` build copies the
  `.so` into the repo root; the upstream install stays intact.
- **gcc 15** requires the `std::min` fix (commit 2) for any build on this box.
